5. DeepCas Architecture & Mathematical Formulation

1. Random Walk Generation
Simulates N_w = 32 paths of length L = 10 over cascade graph G_c(T_obs) weighted by adopter degree.
2. BiGRU & Hierarchical Attention
Embeds nodes (d=50) through BiGRU (h=32). Softmax step attention produces walk vector; walk attention produces cascade representation h_c.
3. MLP Prediction Head
Concatenates h_c with log₂(N_obs + 1) into a 3-layer MLP predicting log₂(ΔP + 1) optimized via AdamW (lr=1e-3).
